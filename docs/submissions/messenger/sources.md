## May quote

### OECD — SME labour productivity (`official`)

In 2024, across 33 OECD and accession-candidate countries, unweighted average labour productivity of SMEs (under 250 persons employed) was **65%** of large firms (250+), business sector, turnover per person employed, nominal. Range: 30% of large-firm productivity in Ireland to 98% in Switzerland. The size gap widened in 20 of 32 countries with a 2013–2024 series.

Lead re-read the primary page on 23 Nov 2026. The abstract and the chapter body match those figures. Costa Rica, Korea, and the United States are 2022 in the underlying note; US SMEs are 1–299 persons. Page date on the chapter: the compendium is the 2026 edition; the data year is 2024.

- Publisher: OECD, *OECD Compendium of Productivity Indicators 2026*, “Labour productivity patterns across firm sizes”.
- URL: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-compendium-of-productivity-indicators-2026_734a5e68-en/full-report/labour-productivity-patterns-across-firm-sizes_7edc4fb8.html
- `as_of`: data year 2024; page re-read 23 Nov 2026.
- Slide use: context that smaller firms produce less turnover per person than large firms. The slide does not say Messenger closes this gap.

### McKinsey Global Institute — time split (`estimate`, 2012)

Interaction workers spend **28%** of the workday on email, **19%** tracking down information, and **14%** collaborating internally. The same page estimates those activities could be done 20–25% more efficiently with social technologies, and names USD 900 billion to 1.3 trillion a year for four sectors.

- Publisher: McKinsey, “Social media's productivity payoff”, 21 August 2012. Authors named on the page: James Manyika, Michael Chui, Hugo Sarrazin.
- URL: https://www.mckinsey.com/mgi/media-center/social-media-productivity-payoff
- `as_of`: 21 August 2012. Research lane opened the page 23 Nov 2026. Lead did not re-fetch it in this pass.
- Slide use: the 28 / 19 / 14 split, with the words “McKinsey estimate, 2012”. Leave 20–25% and the dollar range off result slides. They are potential, dated 2012.

- URL: https://www.gallup.com/workplace/349484/state-of-the-global-workplace.aspx
