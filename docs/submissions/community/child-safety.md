# Dubai-io Community — Child safety: UAE requirements and proposed under-13 policy (Lane E)

**Status:** planned (revised to D1-D10) — awaiting founder approval. Lane E was read-only (web research + repo reads); access date for all fetches **23 Nov 2026**. Founder ruling: D9 — all ages with an age gate, age band self-declared and never inferred, under-18 protections by default, and *"if a requirement cannot be met by 18 Dec, it stops and asks."* This file is the sourcing D9 required before CM-B3.

## Top finding — STOP AND ASK (D9's own clause)

Lane E's official-source evidence conflicts with two parts of D9 as written, and the lead corroborated the headline independently on the UAE Government portal (fetched 200 OK):

> "Children under 15 are not allowed to create, use or operate personal accounts on social media platforms… It is prohibited to collect or use personal data of children under the age of 13 unless certain strict conditions are fulfilled." — [u.ae Children's digital safety](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/social-affairs/Priority-groups/children/Childrens-digital-safety) (updated 03 Nov 2026)

1. **Self-declaration is not sufficient** age verification for social-media platforms (Cabinet Resolution No. 106 of 2026, per official portal + law-firm readings of the Arabic text).
2. **Under-15s may not hold personal social-media accounts at all; parental consent cannot override the ban.** An under-13 policy that admits under-13s to social surfaces with parental consent would therefore be unlawful on the fetched evidence.

**Compliant path adopted in this revision (does not silently override D9):** the 18 Dec demo is **adults-only at the gate** — which D9/D10 already require for testers (≥ 5 consenting adults, sign-up-disabled staging) — with D9's under-18 protections planned in full for any post-demo all-ages launch. Under-15 accounts, the 15–<16 restricted tier, and compliant age verification are **deferred and escalated** (see `open-questions.md`, new blocking item N1). If the founder wants any under-18 account in the 18 Dec demo, Lane E's instruction applies: **stop and ask, with legal advice on the platform-classification question first.**

## Proposed under-13 policy (requirement-mapped)

This replaces "admit under-13s with parental consent" on all social surfaces; under-15 is answered with it because R3 makes 13–14 the harder case.

- **Sign-up (demo, 18 Dec): adults only.** Age screen for every account; sign-up disabled staging with verified 18+ consenting adult testers only (D9/D10). No under-18 account creation in the demo build. — Exceeds R2/R3 for the demo population. Self-declared DOB may route verification, never *be* the verification (R4).
- **Post-demo, if all-ages ambition stands:** under-15 → no personal account, no profile; plain "you must be 15 or older" screen with MoI 116111/Hemayati signpost; collect nothing beyond what age verification strictly needs. No parent-consent override flow (R3). 15–<16 → restricted tier; 16–17 → child protections to 18; 18+ → standard. Requires an approved verification method (R4) — **cannot-meet-by-18-Dec: stop and ask (N1).**
- **Under-13 data outside a social account** (e.g. a future parent-managed listing): only with explicit, documented, verifiable parent/guardian consent, purpose explained, one-tap withdrawal, no ads/profiling (R2). No such surface is in D9 — **do not build one for 18 Dec.**
- **Any permitted minor account (15–17):** highest-privacy default, private profile (D9), no public indexing, no "people you may know" of minors to unknown adults, no intensive algorithmic recommendation to 15–<16, no behavioural advertising or commercial profiling of any child, closed groups only for 15–<16 with caregiver-visible membership and D9 moderator approval, Messenger hand-off applies the same tier or is disabled for the band.
- **Memory/personalisation:** under-18 — no memory writes, no cross-app personalisation (D9 verbatim), enforced server-side in CM-B7/CM-B8.
- **Moderation:** reports involving a minor jump the queue (D9); CSAM/exploitation → immediate removal consideration + immediate report to authorities (116111 / MoI CPC; R5/R9) with the report record preserved.
- **Retention/deletion:** verification artefacts deleted immediately after verification, never reused (R4); minor data purpose-limited, erased without undue delay on withdrawal/discovery of underage account (R8 Arts. 5/15; suspend first, then the written remediation path).
- **Parent controls (15–<16, optional 16–17):** caregiver dashboard that can tighten but never loosen statutory floors — privacy, feature access, daily/night limits, rest periods — plus in-product digital-awareness material. **Not buildable for 18 Dec** alongside compliant verification.

## 18 Dec verdict

- **Can proceed:** adult-only, sign-up-disabled staging demo with ≥ 5 consenting adult testers via the founder's-PC tunnel (D10). Meets every fetched requirement for that population.
- **STOP AND ASK (cannot meet by 18 Dec):** (a) any under-18 — especially under-15/under-13 — account on social surfaces; (b) compliant age verification (approved provider / government ID / AI estimation, TDRA-auditable, Arabic obligations, unpublished standards); (c) the 15–<16 restricted tier + caregiver tooling.
