A blanket “00:40 means Metro stopped” is therefore unsafe without a service date. Its timeline says January registration close, while its FAQ says December. Repository leads: `PLAN.md:16`, `Dubai Visitor Pain Points Research.md:178`; checked on [Dubai Chambers](https://www.dubaichambers.com/en/web/dubai-chamber-of-digital-economy/create-ai-agents). ## Product direction: the three rulings

- **Merchant flow: fixed order. Quote/bid is ridiculous for this MVP and rejected** (founder instruction, user-supplied; independently endorsed as a scope judgment). Latest `ARCHITECTURE.md:124`, `PLAN.md:30` and `docs/DECISIONS.md:7` already reflect this decision. Compare eligible nearby providers with sourced stars/review counts and comparable price-from; omit unavailable ratings as the demo does (`demo/app.js:230`). Show the exact service, quantity, fees and total before the single order approval. Deadline feasibility precedes distance; changing a fulfilment time is an order amendment, not price bidding. The decision log's claim about independent-shop WhatsApp pricing is not adequately sourced by these reports (`docs/DECISIONS.md:7`, `Deepseek-research.md:47`).
- **Execution layer: deterministic host actions, with optional merchant HTML.** Keep the mini-app mechanism for richer category interaction, not as a mandatory platform bet or the agent's execution engine. The host owns catalog validation, approval, state and audit (`ARCHITECTURE.md:40`, `:154`, `:159`). A branded configurable laundry template can demonstrate two merchants without creating two integration contracts (`docs/VISION.md:27`). I disagree with calling per-merchant HTML the core differentiator (`docs/DECISIONS.md:26`): no supplied research tests that preference, while `PLAN.md:68` identifies deadline reasoning as the agency. Prove the companion using standard cards first, then retain HTML where it helps.
- **Positioning: companion first, services last in the pitch, reachable when needed in the app.** Lead with the remembered person and close on the memory payoff; show service ordering as the means of helping them (`PLAN.md:12`, `:59`). Do not turn the pitch into airport navigation + marketplace + website generator. The concierge question remains open; I disagree with treating the hero's demand risk as resolved by desk research or optional validation (`docs/DECISIONS.md:8`, `docs/VISITORS.md:33`). Keep laundry as an explicitly unvalidated showcase until a visitor incident and a real shop establish the gap.

## Critical — resolve before implementing the merchant loop

- **C1: The central deadline behavior has no complete contract.** `PLAN.md:30`/`:56` require promised ready-time, conflict detection, an earlier-time request and merchant confirmation, but `ARCHITECTURE.md:127`–`:140` defines only status transitions. Specify `needed_by` and `promised_ready_at` with dates/timezone, merchant revisions, an approved amendment command, timeout and refusal behavior. A proposed future ready-time must not mark the order `READY` (actually ready). Define what initial approval authorizes; additional disclosure or a material amendment still needs consent (`CLAUDE.md:44`). This is an implementation blocker, not a claim of a deployed incident.

## High

- **H1: Sandbox authentication prescription is incompatible with the prescribed frame.** `ARCHITECTURE.md:157` removes `allow-same-origin`, while `:159` demands strict origin checks on both sides. Such a frame has an opaque origin, so checking the child's origin against its widget URL will reject it ([HTML Standard](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/dev/iframe-embed-object.html), official technical specification). Authenticate the active frame/session with source identity and a per-mount channel/nonce, validate every payload, and check the host origin in the child. Do not add `allow-same-origin` to fix this; the demo's source check is a starting point, not a complete session protocol (`demo/app.js:263`).
- **H2: The host trusts widget prices and payload shapes.** Scope checking at `demo/app.js:273` is followed by forwarding the payload to approval (`:281`); `:329` injects unvalidated quantity into HTML and `:332` labels `p.estimate` a fixed total. Palm Leaf explicitly says estimate before delivery (`demo/miniapps/palmleaf.html:29`). A same-frame payload can falsify price, break rendering or inject markup through quantity. Rebuild totals from approved catalog/version and typed item IDs, validate bounds, render untrusted fields as text, and bind approval to the validated snapshot (`ARCHITECTURE.md:91`). Demo-local today; unsafe to reuse as the production boundary.
- **H3: Hotel area leaves the host before sharing approval.** On `app.ready`, `demo/app.js:267`–`:269` sends `state.dest` into merchant HTML; the approval appears only at `:330`. That contradicts the promised disclosure boundary (`CLAUDE.md:44`, `PLAN.md:55`). No-network fixtures limit the present exposure, but the host should request consent before disclosing personal context or keep it host-side.
- **H4: Blank inputs manufacture “user-supplied” memories.** Empty belt becomes `7` (`demo/app.js:132`), empty taxi becomes `12345` (`:166`), and empty bag description becomes a backpack/laptop (`:406`). Trip start uses the machine's current time and is logged as user-supplied without confirmation (`:167`, `:398`). Require input/confirmation or label prefilled examples `demo_fixture`; this directly undermines the product's trust promise (`CLAUDE.md:41`, `:47`).
- **H5: The sketch lacks the hero's decision.** Laundry free text selects a category without retaining a deadline (`demo/app.js:424`); Fold & Press puts the pickup slot only in `summary` (`demo/miniapps/foldpress.html:34`), but the host replaces that summary with item names (`demo/app.js:329`). Auto-respond proceeds to completion (`:373`) without deadline comparison, earlier-time discussion or refusal. Only one laundry fixture exists (`demo/data.js:112`), versus three laundries/two previews in `PLAN.md:54`. This is an acknowledged sketch backlog (`PLAN.md:89`), but the current demo cannot validate the claimed agent behavior.
- **H6: Recovery stops at inert controls and incomplete memory.** Taxi storage omits roof colour, receipt and explicit trip date/drop-off time (`demo/app.js:167`); “Contact official” and “Copy report” have no handlers (`:409`). The promised morning check-in has no reminder implementation (`:411`). Implement an honest copy/link handoff, ask for missing fields and obtain reminder consent; preserve “not recovered” (`PLAN.md:31`, `CLAUDE.md:46`).
- **H7: Simulated fulfilment is insufficiently labelled.** Auto-respond starts checked (`demo/index.html:121`); status cards omit a simulation/fixture pill and generate a random reference (`demo/app.js:350`, `:367`). “Shared with family” is shown after a log entry alone (`:175`). Use a persistent “Scripted demo” label, “Merchant role, simulated,” and fixture labels on status/reference cards as prescribed (`PLAN.md:50`, `:61`); do not imply a real send.
- **H8: Ride reasoning ignores time and terminal.** All options remain selectable at `demo/app.js:143`–`:154`; fixture terminal is always T3 (`demo/data.js:5`). Specify weekday/date, terminal and verified schedule inputs; handle Friday's after-midnight service and avoid promising the last train from general operating hours (top-five fact 3; `PLAN.md:28`).
- **H9: The evidence does not settle hotel competition or merchant participation.** The underlying gaps are explicitly recorded (`docs/VISITORS.md:33`, `:62`), but the research report generalizes concierge failure from unsuitable sources (`Dubai Visitor Pain Points Research.md:51`, `:243`). Do not claim hotels fail generally or that merchants prefer a new inbox. Validate the concrete overnight deadline and existing workaround before committing to the full platform.
