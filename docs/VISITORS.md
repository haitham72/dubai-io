# Visitor needs: hypotheses to test

**Status:** hypothesis inventory, **not research**. Nothing here is evidence of frequency, severity or demand, including the archived case studies (they are plausible stories) and earlier LLM reviews (they are opinions).

**Working story (not validated):** short-stay business visitor → independent laundry with a deadline → taxi-memory payoff.
**Next milestone:** evidence that visitors struggle with **coordination and follow-through**, and **prefer our experience over the help they already use**, including the hotel.

Tags: `[T03]` / `[S11]` = archived scenario (`archive/handoff/06` Part II, `03`), a hypothesis · `[fact?]` = factual claim to check against official sources (interviews can't verify facts) · `[discovery]` = only interviews can answer.

## 1. The hardest open question: the hotel concierge

For a short-stay business visitor, **the hotel may already handle shirt pressing and taxi problems.** If it does this well, our hero story has no gap. Interviews must establish:

- How often visitors use the hotel for these tasks, and what it costs them (price, time, availability at night)
- **Where hotel help falls short:** deadlines missed, no follow-up, lost items after leaving the hotel's taxi rank, budget hotels/apartments with no real concierge, needs that happen outside the hotel
- Whether the visitor would choose Dubai-io, their previous approach or the hotel *for the same incident*

If the hotel wins in most interviews, change the story (e.g. serviced-apartment and Airbnb guests, or needs away from the hotel) before building more.

## 5. Interview script (6–8 short-stay business visitors, ~20 min)

**Rules:** ask about **real past incidents**, not opinions or the future. Don't give examples before they answer (no priming). Ask "what did you do, how long did it take, what did it cost, did it get resolved?"

**Part 1: before showing anything**

1. Tell me about your most recent work trip to Dubai, or a similar city, from landing to the hotel. What happened?
2. **Tell me about the last time you needed something arranged before a specific time during a trip.**
3. **Who handled it: you, the hotel, a colleague or an app? What remained difficult?**
4. **Did you have to repeat information or chase a reply? Walk me through that.**
5. On that trip, did anything go wrong? *(no examples)* What did you do, how long did it take, what did it cost, and was it resolved?
6. What did the hotel help with? Where did it not help?

**Part 2: show the 4-minute demo, then**

7. In one sentence, what does it do?
8. What did it remember about the traveller? Who confirmed the laundry request?
9. **For that same incident you described, which would you choose: Dubai-io, your previous approach, or the hotel? Why?**

## 6. How to record findings

One row per interview: persona fit · incident described · frequency signal · consequence (time / money / stress) · workaround and who · hotel role · choice in Q9 and why.

**Reading the results:** three unprompted mentions of something is a **signal to investigate**, not confirmation. A finding becomes a decision only when the frequency, consequence and workaround all point the same way, and Q9 shows people prefer our approach to what they already use.
