## Who Can Join

Open to anyone ready to solve a real problem with an AI Agent, including:

- Students
- Tech enthusiasts
- Entrepreneurs
- Innovators
- App experts

Participants may come from around the world. The Championship welcomes both technical and non-technical participants.

- **No minimum age** to enter the Championship.
- Participants entering the **Youth Category** must be **under 21 years of age at the time of registration**.

---

## Participation Rules

- Individual and team entries are accepted.
- **Maximum team size: 6.**
- Each entry must present an original AI Agent solution from any industry or sector.
- The AI Agent submitted must be at the **idea, prototype, or early MVP stage** and must not already be a fully commercialized solution.
- Existing businesses may apply if they are proposing a new AI Agent solution or capability.
- While applications may start at the idea, prototype, or early MVP stage, finalists reaching the Grand Final must present a **fully functional, working AI Agent** on stage.
- The AI Agent must not be copied or taken from an existing repository.
- The solution must protect personal, confidential, and organizational data and prevent information leakage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

- **Q: What is an AI Agent?**  
  A: An AI Agent is more than a chatbot. It can understand a goal, make decisions, use tools, and take action to complete a task.

- **Q: How is an AI Agent different from traditional AI?**  
  A: Unlike traditional AI, which usually responds to a single prompt, an AI Agent can plan multiple steps, connect with other systems, learn from results, and keep working towards an outcome.

- **Q: What is the challenge?**  
  A: Your challenge is to turn a real problem into a functional AI Agent with the potential to become a useful product, service, or scalable venture.

- **Q: Who can participate?**  
  A: The AI Agents Championship is open to everyone regardless of age or location, whether in the UAE or anywhere else in the world. This includes students, tech enthusiasts, entrepreneurs, innovators, and app development experts. The Youth Category is reserved for entries led by a participant under 21 years of age at the time of registration.

- **Q: How do I get started?**  
  A: First, register for the Championship to access the platform, learning videos, workshops, and other participant resources. You will be notified when submissions open.

- **Q: When does the Championship take place?**  
  A: The Championship begins with registration and learning activities in **October 2026**. Registrations close in **January 2027**, semi-finalists are announced in **January 2027**, and the competition concludes with the Grand Final in the **first week of May 2027**.

- **Q: Is there a participation fee?**  
  A: No. Participation in the AI Agents Championship is free of charge. Participants are responsible for any costs associated with developing their solution.

- **Q: Do I need technical experience?**  
  A: No. The competition welcomes participants with varying levels of experience. Online courses are offered for beginners, and resources are available for experienced developers to enhance their skills.

- **Q: Can I participate as part of a team?**  
  A: Yes. You can collaborate with others and enter as a team, with a maximum of 6 members per team. Specific team-related guidelines will be provided during registration.

- **Q: Do all team members need to register?**  
  A: During the registration stage (Nov–Dec), each team member must register individually to access the platform and learning resources.

- **Q: Will mentorship be available?**  
  A: Yes. Mentoring sessions and online workshops are offered to support participants throughout the competition, with insights from industry experts and business leaders.

- **Q: Can I submit more than one application?**  
  A: Yes. During the submission stage (Nov–Jan), you can submit more than one application.

- **Q: Do I need to register twice?**  
  A: No. There is no need to register twice as a participant during the enrolment stage (7 Oct–Jan).

- **Q: Do I need a fully working AI Agent to apply?**  
  A: No. You can apply with an idea, prototype, or early-stage MVP. However, if you progress to the Grand Final, you will be expected to present a fully working AI Agent on stage — not just an MVP — demonstrating live functionality to the judges.

- **Q: In what language must applications be submitted?**  
  A: The registration form and all related submission materials must be submitted in **English only**.

- **Q: What prizes are available?**  
  A: A total of **USD 750,000 in grants** is available. Each category winner will receive a **USD 150,000 grant** to support the development and growth of their AI Agent solution and business. One of the four core category winners will also receive the Championship’s highest recognition — the title of **Dubai AI Agent of the Year** — along with additional funding.
