## Submission and eligibility checklist

- [ ] Complete free participant registration; every team member registers individually (maximum six members).
- [ ] Confirm exact portal submission deadline and submission instructions.
- [ ] Prepare prototype, pitch deck, demo video and explanatory summary report.
- [ ] Submit materials in English.
- [ ] Confirm original ownership, third-party licenses and non-commercialized/new-capability eligibility.
- [ ] Demonstrate data protection and compliance with UAE law; review prohibited political, religious and immoral content.
- [ ] If shortlisted, prepare live demonstration; Grand Final requires a working agent beyond MVP stage.
- [ ] If finalist, attend physically in Dubai, subject to security clearance.
- [ ] If winner, establish or hold a valid Dubai company trade license before receiving funding. No license needed for entry.

Sources: S1–S3. Development costs are the participant's responsibility. Training is recommended, not compulsory. The terms may change.
