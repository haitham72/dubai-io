> Restored 24 Nov by Claude from its verbatim read of the original, after DeepSeek's audit overwrote this filename at 10:13 (DeepSeek's is now `tn_upgrade_audit_deepseek.md`).

## 1. Verdict

**Sound with fixes.** Keep temporal_note and prioritize Postgres retrieval, but make privacy, Forget, and the Postgres-backed bridge release blockers for the Vault.
The isolated 20k-fact SQL checks support a Postgres-first route. The T-1 data supports a limited retrieval baseline and gives no release evidence for graph latency, multilingual extraction, stories, or Rule 9.
Move acceptance to the start. Ship the graph after its visibility and mobile contracts pass. Defer embeddings and generated narrative until measured gains justify them.

## 4. T-1 validity

- **T2b reproduces the saved arithmetic.** The runners feed the same 110 ledger IDs and fact texts to both loaders, plus the same 40 query texts. temporal_note receives IDs, folded text and valid-from/default date, all active, no text_orig/evidence/entity/alias/edge rows and one generic subject `life/general`. The other loader attaches fact ID and a sensitivity flag to fact text, with filtering only by synthetic user. There is no special TN access to gold entities, aliases or actual subjects. “Held equal” describes attempted fact texts and queries. Temporal and sensitivity representations, retrieval budgets and timed paths differ. Evidence: T-1 `run_tn_t2b.py:46–78`, `run_t2b_equal.py:43–62`, `live/context.py:434–477`.
- **Coverage is exact ID intersection.** Average per-probe recall over 38 probes with relevant IDs. `must_not_contain` is a case-insensitive substring check. The raw JSON saves counts and matched strings, so returned rankings and semantic matches cannot be reconstructed. The saved TN aggregates are 0.60658/0.64386, upper-middle latency 11.65 ms (ordinary median 11.60). The embedding loader’s aggregate 0.53509/0.66798 also reproduces. These are run bookkeeping, including the figure used to propose 0.668, with no product selection implication. No judge ran for T2b. The earlier month extraction used a separate judge. Evidence: both T2b `score_hits` functions and output aggregation; `weekbench/score.py:51–69`.
- **T3 and smoke.** The synthetic in-process smoke demonstrates that the retrieval functions returned facts, including a seeded inferred family fact. At 1k/5k/20k, warm p50/p95 from the historical script was 26/27, 175/182 and 287/308 ms. The 20k search median was 231 ms. The old needle was missed by both functions and found by SQL. The 0.8 ms is the median of five unranked `Careem` SQL queries, while needle detection was a separate untimed query. One revision bump explains the 398 ms sample. T6’s 525.0 MiB is a three-container snapshot without the app/worker/model. Evidence: T-1 `proof/temporal_note/smoke.json:2–16`, `raw/temporal_note/t3_latency.txt:2–6`, `proof/t6_docker_stats.txt:2–4`, reference `tn_latency_bench.py:99–126`.

T-1 supplied no full-month production extraction/cost, end-to-end T2, TN Forget, graph/story, or multilingual embedding result.
