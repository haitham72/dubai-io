## /links vs /pair
- /api/identity/links/start|complete: link an additional external identity (issuer/subject JWT) to the same user.
- /api/identity/pair/start|complete: QR/code to give a phone its own session for the signed-in user.
They are different purposes, so not two competing pairing flows.
