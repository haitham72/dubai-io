## Manual isolation sheet (founder, about 5 min, needs the tester Gmail)
1. Run start-all. In browser profile A open http://localhost:3060/signin and Continue with Google as the founder; the page shows your name. Same on http://localhost:5173 (Settings > Account > Continue with Google).
2. In profile B (or a private window) sign in as the tester Gmail on both sites.
3. In A save something with a distinctive word (for example "falconry") in Community or Messenger. In B check Feed, Inbox, search and Chat: the word and your name must never appear. Repeat in reverse.
4. Forget in A (Community Profile > Forget, or a Messenger conversation forget): the item disappears in all three apps for A; B is unchanged.
5. New accounts start with memory off everywhere; nothing is stored until it is switched on.
