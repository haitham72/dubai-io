## Files

- `src/temporal_memory/core/persona.py` (new, ~900 lines) — the single §9 projection. Bounded SQL
  (per-subject `ROW_NUMBER() <= 12` windows, capped entity/place reads, batched `fact_evidence` lookup);
  never loads all facts. Builds `Leaf`s for subjects, tiered people (`inner-circle/` T1,
  `friends-and-peers/` T2, `professional-network/` T3 — confirmed `closeness_tier` only) and places;
  four blocks per leaf (Current Snapshot / Open Loops & Active Signals / Timeline & Fact History /
  Evidence Limits); every bullet carries `fact_ids`, rendered provenance (`You told me` only for
  `user_supplied`), `as_of`, and `was … until …` for superseded/ended facts.
- `src/temporal_memory/api/cards.py` (new) — `GET /v1/owners/{o}/cards?cluster=|person=` and
  `GET /v1/owners/{o}/people/{entity_id}/card` (person card: relation, confirmed tier vs "I noticed"
  suggestion, snapshot, shared subjects, recent changes). Mounted in `api/app.py`.
- `src/temporal_memory/api/export_md.py` (rewritten) — renders the §9 tree: root files `USER.md`
  (7 sections citing leaves), `NOW.md` (open loops, next-30-day dates, rising topics, pending
  confirmations), `MEMORIES.md` (index + counts), `SOURCES.md` (watermarks, evidence limits,
  Never-Remember rules, held-back counts), `MECHANICAL_INDEX.md` (subject → evidence record ids, no
  LLM), `_staging/hypotheses-to-confirm.md`, `_staging/proposed-tree-splits.md` (proposal only at
  ≥ 12 facts, never auto-applied).
- `tests/test_persona_cards.py` (new) — Postgres happy + failure paths (below).
- Updated: `tests/test_export.py`, `tests/test_export_cli_and_model_observed.py`,
  `tests/test_reflect.py`, `tests/test_surfaces_postgres.py`, `tests/e2e/test_gold_month.py`.

## Privacy / truth behaviour (verified by tests)

- Sensitive subjects (`sensitive_cat`, `health`/`faith`/`wellbeing` roots) never get a leaf or bullet;
  counts appear only under held-back in `SOURCES.md`/Evidence Limits. Health/faith are never a spine.
- Quarantined items appear as category counts only — no record text.
- `inferred` facts render as `inferred`, never `You told me`; `user_supplied` renders `You told me`.
- `hypothesis` facts land in `noticed` + Open Loops + `_staging/hypotheses-to-confirm.md` with
  Confirm / Not true actions; unconfirmed tier suggestions render as "I noticed", not a tier.
- People & family leaves use stated/confirmed provenance only.
- Forgotten facts (`erased_at`, stale `record_generation`, matching `forget_rules`) disappear from
  tree and cards on the next render (privacy revision respected — no cache).
- Superseded/ended facts render `was <text> until <valid_to>` in Timeline & Fact History.
- Split proposal fires at ≥ 12 facts on a subject leaf and is never applied.

## Notes for the integrator

- `cluster_for_path` maps stored subject roots onto the 7 spines; unmapped roots are dropped from the
  tree (never silently re-homed). `interests` currently routes to `06-events` — flag if the founder
  wants a different home.
- Person/place leaves cap fact windows at 12 rows; `fact_count` on person leaves is the window count,
  on subject leaves the true SQL `COUNT(*)`.
- `/cards?person=<id>` only returns cards for tier-confirmed people (they are the only person leaves);
  suggested-only people resolve through `/people/{entity_id}/card` which renders the "I noticed" tier.
