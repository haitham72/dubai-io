## 2. Architecture & D18 Compliance

- **Level-by-level BFS expansion (`vocab/loader.py`):** high-weight learned terms (`> 0.05`) first, curated aliases round-robin across siblings at each depth so no child starves, and floor-weight learned terms (`<= 0.05`) pushed last within `max_terms=40`. Leaf tokens never expand siblings.
- **Per-owner cache (`core/retrieval.py`):** `_LEARNED_CACHE` keyed by `(id(db), owner)` and invalidated automatically via `owners.facts_version` bump.
- **Same-call LLM proposals (`core/extract.py`, `core/applier.py`):** optional `new_terms: [{"term", "is_a", "evidence"}]` added cleanly without pasting the tree into the prompt. Backend validates `no_source`, `ambiguous`, `no_branch`, `rule9` (inherited ancestor flags + `eligible_subject` + `pre_write`), and `curated_clash`, persisting accepted terms in `learned_terms` + `learned_term_sources` (`db/migrations/0007_learned_terms.sql`). Second sightings append sources without duplicating `learned_terms`.
- **Owner visibility (`api/vault.py`):** added `GET /v1/owners/{owner}/vocab/learned` returning `{owner, items, count, provenance, as_of}` (each item includes `term`, `term_folded`, `parent_path`, `path`, `provenance`, `weight`, `source_record_ids`, `created_at`), gated by `require_owner_access` (`403` for cross-owner or worker tokens).
- **D18 weight lowering (no deletions):** `weight` on `learned_terms` is dynamically derived from the maximum effective weight of citing live facts / records (rejected `"Not true"` facts via `had_fact` or erased records drop that source to `WEIGHT_FLOOR = 0.05`; single-source drop puts the term at floor while keeping the row; two sources retain the higher weight). In `compute_subject_salience_sql` (`core/retrieval.py`), inferred unconfirmed subjects whose facts all sit at `<= 0.05` sit at the `0.05` salience floor without deleting any `subjects` or `subject_aliases` rows (§E #18 D18). `privacy/forget.py` has zero diff.
