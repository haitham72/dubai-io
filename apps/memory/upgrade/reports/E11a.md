# E11a - End cues and late supersession

25 Nov 2026 / Part A only / `e11a/temporal-fixes` / base `5c67119` / implementation `2d6af51`

## Changes

- **A1.** The fallback requires an unnegated owner action or an ending of an owner-possessed event. It checks English, Egyptian and Gulf Arabic, and Arabizi negation in both the rendered fact and the original text. A named third party ending their own event stays active. The handling of explicit `end` operations is unchanged.
- **A2.** A late fact becomes history when newer active evidence changes the same schedule or price. The fallback compares the event or item identity and its typed value. An unlabelled rent statement also needs corroboration from the same source and sender. Separate trips, bookings and expenses stay active. Edits in `core/applier.py` are limited to `_late_superseder` and its helpers.
